## Inspired by TASCAM's original
The layout and behaviour follow TASCAM's own US-16x08 Settings Panel. No TASCAM code or artwork is
included. Facts such as parameter ranges and EQ frequency steps were checked against the original
panel and TASCAM's Reference Manual so the numbers match.

## Built with Claude
Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic (https://www.anthropic.com), did much of the hard work:
analysing the original panel and the Linux driver, working out how the unit talks over USB, and
writing the code, tests and documentation.

## App icon
`Resources/AppIcon*` is original artwork generated by `tools/make_icon.py` (GPL-2.0-or-later like the rest of the
project). It is an homage to the "bio-cell" panel style of TASCAM's original Settings Panel (brushed metal around
rounded black cells) but the pattern is our own and no TASCAM artwork is used.
